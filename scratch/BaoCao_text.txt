FitnessIn 2026, digital transformation - driven by AI, IoT, computer vision, and cloud computing - is revolutionizing patient monitoring and emergency response. Modern systems continuously analyze patient behavior and vitals to detect critical events like falls or cardiac arrest, instantly alerting caregivers. Increased accessibility of smart devices and high-speed internet makes these solutions highly practical for hospitals, nursing homes, and home care. A key emerging trend is integrating automated anomaly detection with real-time, intelligent first-aid guidance. Consequently, developing an AI-powered Fall and Cardiac Arrest Detection System with Intelligent First Aid Support is highly relevant, directly addressing current technological trends and safety needs.Existing Practical ProblemsCurrently, many elderly care environments, hospitals, and home-care systems still face major challenges in continuously monitoring patients and responding quickly to emergency situations. In particular, elderly individuals and patients with high-risk medical conditions may experience dangerous incidents without immediate assistance.Some common practical problems include:Difficulty in monitoring patients continuously in real time, especially during nighttime or in private home-care environments.Delayed detection of dangerous situations such as falls, unconsciousness, seizures, or abnormal body movements.Lack of automated emergency response systems capable of immediately notifying caregivers or family members when accidents occur.High dependence on manual supervision, which may lead to delayed reactions and human-related errors.Existing monitoring systems often provide only basic surveillance functions without intelligent behavior analysis.Limited integration between AI-based detection, real-time alert systems, and emergency communication pipelines.Advanced healthcare monitoring solutions are often expensive and difficult to deploy widely in small healthcare facilities or home-care environments.Furthermore, existing solutions often focus on single-function monitoring, such as basic CCTV or simple fall detection, lacking multi-functional detection, real-time alert pipelines, evidence recording, and AI caregiver support.Consequently, developing an intelligent healthcare platform that integrates real-time video analysis, multi-scenario AI emergency detection, automated alerts, and smart assistance has become a critical requirement in modern care settings.Market and User DemandThe demand for intelligent healthcare monitoring and emergency response systems is rapidly increasing, particularly in hospitals, nursing homes, rehabilitation centers, and home-care environments. As the number of elderly individuals and high-risk patients continues to grow, healthcare providers and families increasingly require smart technologies capable of improving patient safety and reducing emergency response time.Users and healthcare organizations expect modern healthcare monitoring systems to provide:Real-time patient monitoring through continuous video surveillance and intelligent behavior analysis.Automatic detection of dangerous situations such as falls, unconsciousness, seizures, and abnormal body movements.Immediate emergency alert mechanisms capable of notifying caregivers, medical staff, or family members in real time.Intelligent emergency support features that assist caregivers during critical situations.Integrated monitoring platforms that combine AI detection, alert systems, evidence recording, and remote monitoring into a single system.Easy-to-use and cost-effective solutions suitable for both healthcare facilities and home-care environments.AI-powered technologies capable of improving detection accuracy while minimizing false alarms.In addition, the development of artificial intelligence, computer vision, real-time streaming technologies, and smart healthcare systems has created favorable conditions for building more intelligent and efficient emergency monitoring platforms.These demands demonstrate the strong practical potential of developing the Cardiac Alert System (CAS), an AI-powered healthcare monitoring platform designed to provide real-time emergency detection, intelligent alert pipelines, and smart assistance features. The system aims to improve emergency response efficiency, enhance patient safety, and support modern digital healthcare environments through integrated intelligent technologies.Related Technology TrendsThe rapid development of modern technologies such as:Artificial Intelligence (AI),Machine Learning,Deep Learning,Computer Vision,Real-time Streaming Technologies,Cloud Computing,Mobile Application Development,Internet of Things (IoT),has created significant opportunities for developing intelligent healthcare monitoring systems capable of automatically detecting dangerous situations and supporting emergency response processes in real time.AI-powered technologies are transforming smart healthcare and elderly care. Modern solutions leverage computer vision and deep learning to analyze patient behavior and detect emergencies like falls, unconsciousness, or abnormal movements in real time.Simultaneously, advancements in real-time communication, cloud services, and mobile platforms enable remote monitoring and rapid response capabilities. Modern systems are also incorporating AI assistants and automated alert pipelines to optimize interaction between patients, caregivers, and healthcare providers.Human pose estimation, activity recognition, and real-time anomaly detection represent major research frontiers. These trends form the foundation of the Cardiac Alert System (CAS), which consolidates AI video analysis, automated alerting, and intelligent assistance into a single healthcare platform.Objectives and ContributionsGeneral ObjectiveThe project develops the Cardiac Alert System (CAS), an AI-powered platform providing continuous emergency monitoring for elderly and high-risk patients in healthcare and home-care settings. It delivers a scalable ecosystem integrating computer vision detection (falls, seizures, unconsciousness), live dashboards, and automated alert pipelines.Specific ObjectivesThe project focuses on achieving the following objectives:Analyze requirements and apply computer vision techniques for real-time patient activity analysis.Develop AI models to detect falls, seizures, unconsciousness, and prolonged inactivity using pose estimation.Build a real-time, multi-channel alert pipeline and a live monitoring dashboard for caregivers.Integrate a RAG-based AI assistant for medical information retrieval and first-aid guidance.Design and deploy a responsive MVP prototype with user-friendly web and mobile interfaces.Evaluate system stability through practical testing and outline future directions for smart elderly care.Contributions of the ProjectProposing an integrated healthcare monitoring platform that combines MediaPipe-based pose estimation, deep learning, and real-time emergency response.Developing a CNN-LSTM model for skeletal motion analysis to identify critical events like falls, seizures, and unconsciousness.Creating an automated alert pipeline utilizing WebSockets, Telegram bot notifications, and incident video archiving.Integrating a RAG-based AI assistant to retrieve medical information and offer real-time emergency guidance.Building a web dashboard supporting live video streams, incident history, and patient monitoring management.Providing a modular and scalable architecture ready for expansion into elderly care, hospital surveillance, and smart-home safety networks.Scope of the ProjectTarget UsersThe target users of the system include:·  An AI-powered healthcare monitoring system for real-time emergency detection.·  A web-based monitoring dashboard for healthcare management and emergency visualization.·  A backend system for real-time video processing, emergency event management, and alert handling.·  An AI inference system for human pose estimation and emergency activity recognition.·  A RAG-based intelligent healthcare assistant for healthcare information retrieval and emergency support.·  A database system for storing healthcare data, camera information, and emergency event logs.Technology ScopeThe project focuses on developing:·  An AI-powered healthcare monitoring system for real-time emergency detection.·  A web-based monitoring dashboard for healthcare management and emergency visualization.·  A backend system for real-time video processing, emergency event management, and alert handling.·  An AI inference system for human pose estimation and emergency activity recognition.·  A RAG-based intelligent healthcare assistant for healthcare information retrieval and emergency support.·  A database system for storing healthcare data, camera information, and emergency event logs.Technologies expected to be used include:·  Next.js and TypeScript for web dashboard development.·  Go (Golang) and Gin Framework for backend services and real-time communication.·  MongoDB and Redis for database and state management.·  Python, PyTorch, and MediaPipe for AI processing and human pose estimation.·  CNN-LSTM models for emergency activity recognition.·  YOLO for furniture and object detection to reduce false alarms.·  FastAPI and ChromaDB for the RAG-based AI assistant system.·  WebSocket, FFmpeg, and HLS streaming technologies for real-time communication and video streaming.·  Telegram Bot API and Android ADB integration for emergency alert mechanisms.System LimitationsWithin the scope of this project, the system does not focus on:Direct medical diagnosis or replacement of professional healthcare services.Advanced biometric monitoring through specialized medical devices.Real-time physiological signal monitoring such as ECG or blood pressure analysis.Large-scale hospital management functionalities.Fully autonomous emergency medical decision-making systems.The system mainly focuses on AI-based emergency detection, healthcare monitoring, and automated emergency alert support through computer vision technologies.Deployment EnvironmentThe system is expected to be deployed on:Web-based monitoring platforms accessible through modern browsers.Cloud or VPS environments for backend services and AI processing.Home-care and elderly care monitoring environments using IP cameras or RTSP-supported cameras.Hospitals, nursing homes, and rehabilitation centers requiring real-time emergency monitoring systems.Local network and internet-connected environments supporting real-time communication and video streaming technologies.Practical SignificancePractical ApplicationsThis project helps address practical challenges in healthcare monitoring and emergency response, particularly for elderly individuals and high-risk patients requiring continuous observation.
